# DegreeFlow 🎓

> **Plan your degree. Visualize your path. Build your future.**

DegreeFlow is a prototype for an interactive academic planning platform designed to help university students understand and navigate their degree pathways.

Instead of viewing a degree as a list of courses and requirements, DegreeFlow turns it into a **visual, interactive roadmap**—making it easier for students to see where they are, what comes next, and how different academic decisions can affect their journey.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Interactive Degree Map** — Visualize your degree as a connected academic pathway.
- 📚 **Course Planning** — See completed, current, and upcoming courses in one place.
- 🔄 **Explore Alternatives** — Experiment with different academic pathways and course choices.
- 🎓 **Major & Minor Exploration** — Explore how changing your major or adding a minor could affect your degree.
- 🏫 **University Selection** — Explore degree pathways across different universities.
- 📊 **Progress Overview** — Get a clear picture of your academic progress and remaining requirements.
- 🌙 **Modern Interface** — A dark, cinematic interface with glassmorphism, gradients, and interactive visual elements.

## 💡 The Problem

University degree planning can be surprisingly difficult (trust me i know). 

Students often have to piece together information from course catalogs, degree plans, prerequisite lists, university websites, and academic advising. This can make it difficult to understand the **bigger picture** of their degree.

Having to constantly think about: 

- *What courses should I take next?*
- *What happens if I change my major?*
- *Can I add a minor without delaying graduation?*
- *Which courses are prerequisites for my future courses?*
- *What will my degree look like if I take a different path?*

This can be soo tiring and unnecessarily stressful.

### The Solution

**DegreeFlow transforms degree requirements into an interactive visual experience.**

Rather than simply reading a checklist, students can **see their academic journey**, explore different possibilities, and make more informed decisions about their degree.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

DegreeFlow was designed around the idea of a:

> **"Living map of your degree."**

The interface uses:

- Dark, cinematic visuals
- Glassmorphism
- Gradient borders and glow effects
- Interactive course cards
- Connected pathway visualizations
- Smooth transitions and animations
- Minimal, modern academic aesthetics

The goal is to make academic planning feel less like navigating a boring spreadsheet and more like **exploring a personalized pathway**.

## 🧪 Current Status

DegreeFlow is currently a **design prototype / early-stage project** created for a university designathon.

The current version focuses primarily on demonstrating the **user experience, visual design, and concept** of an interactive degree-planning platform.

Future versions could include:

- Real university degree databases
- Automated prerequisite checking
- Graduation timeline prediction
- Personalized course and elective recommendations
- GPA and workload planning
- More universities and degree programs
- AI-powered academic pathway suggestions

## 🎯 Vision

DegreeFlow aims to make academic planning more **visual, transparent, and student-friendly**.

Instead of asking students to figure out their degree requirements on their own, DegreeFlow puts the entire pathway in front of them—allowing them to explore **"what if?"** scenarios before making academic decisions.
