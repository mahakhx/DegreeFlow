The problem

Your degree isn't
a straight line.

But most academic planning tools treat it like one.

Students are constantly making decisions that change their academic path — switching majors, adding minors, rearranging courses, changing universities, or trying to graduate earlier. Yet understanding the consequences of those decisions often means manually comparing requirements, prerequisites, credits and course schedules.

Explore Your Path → See the evidence ↓

One degree. Many possible paths. Most tools show you only the first one.

The gap

The problem isn't knowing what you have.
It's knowing what happens next.

Most degree audit systems are built to answer one question well. Students are asking a different one.

What a degree audit answers

  • Completed courses
  • Remaining requirements
  • GPA
  • Credits earned

A snapshot of one fixed path — accurate, and static.

What students actually ask

What if I add a minor?

To answer these, students work through course catalogues, prerequisite lists, degree audits, advisor appointments, spreadsheets and trial and error.

Why changing university is not simple

The UAE Ministry of Education's inspection framework requires that undergraduate transfer credits be awarded only for courses relevant to the receiving degree, and that credit is not granted twice for the same course taken at two institutions. Which is exactly why a student needs to see how their completed courses map onto a different degree before they commit.

Framework for Compliance Inspection of HEIs, 2023 (metric 7.4.5) ↗
